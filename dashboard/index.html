How the value is estimated

  1. Calibrate. 128 protocols in the snapshot record a token with a market cap. For each, the model takes the ratio of market cap to TVL — the multiple the market already pays.
  2. Fit the curve. Protocols are grouped into six bands by TVL; the median multiple in each band is fitted with a monotone (never rising) curve, so bigger protocols are never credited with a richer multiple than smaller ones.
  3. Adjust. The curve value is corrected with the median residual for the protocol’s category, its seven-day TVL growth quartile, how long it has run, and how many chains it spans. Corrections come from the calibration protocols only.
  4. Band it. The 80% band is the 10th–90th range of out-of-fold residuals: honest about how wide real multiples run.

Honest validation

Median error of the implied multiple, the model refitted from scratch on each split. The baseline predicts every protocol at the global median multiple.

The fitted curve

Hollow dots are the median multiple in each of the six TVL bands; the gold line is the monotone curve fitted through them (neighbouring bands pooled where a median rose). The ring marks the protocol in the detail view.

The board covers the snapshot’s whole watchlist and ranks pre-token protocols the watchlist leaves out. Protocols whose token already trades are used only to calibrate the curve — they never appear on the board.

Data: DefiLlama protocol snapshot, frozen at the fetch named above; every figure on this page comes from that file. “Records no token” means the snapshot carries no token record for the protocol — it may still have one. Implied values are estimates from a statistical model, not offers, advice, or a forecast of any launch.
